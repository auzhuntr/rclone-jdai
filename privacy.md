# Privacy policy: rclone-jdai

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

**rclone-jdai** is a private tool used only by its developer, James Leake, to synchronise the developer's own files between the developer's own computers.

## What the app accesses

The app connects to the developer's own Google Drive account using Google's Drive API, to read, write and list files in a folder the developer chose, so that files stay in step between the developer's computers.

## What data is collected and where it goes

- The app does **not** collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to any third party.
- Files and the sign-in token stay on the developer's own computers and in the developer's own Google Drive.
- There is no website analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The app is not used by anyone other than its developer.

## Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

The developer can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
